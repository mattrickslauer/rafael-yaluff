About

Painterly research in motion

Rafael Yaluff is a visual artist working across painting and image-making, drawing from urban rhythm, memory, and personal mythology. His practice explores how a place can carry emotional residue — how surfaces, gestures, and silence can tell a story.

Through layered color and uneasy compositions, he builds scenes that feel both intimate and unstable, searching for clarity in contradiction.
